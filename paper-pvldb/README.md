# Paper 1, PVLDB Vol. 20 version (Experiment, Analysis & Benchmark track)

This folder holds a 12-page PVLDB submission condensed from Paper 1 (`../paper/`, 38 pages).
Paper 1 is cited inside the paper as its extended version, and Paper 2 (`../paper2/`) as a
companion manuscript. The number discipline is Paper 1's: every result is a `\stat{}` macro.
`../paper/tools/stats_to_tex.py` fills those macros from the committed `stats.json` files, and no
result is typed into `main.tex`. Build steps and the hashes of the reproducible build are in
`FINAL_BUILD.md`.

## Files

| Path | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `main.tex` | The paper (acmart `sigconf,nonacm` + `pvldb.sty`, as in the official template) |
| `macros.tex` | **Verbatim copy** of `../paper/macros.tex` (`\stat`, host flags, TPC disclaimer). `make check` fails if they differ |
| `pvldb-macros.tex` | Additions for this version only: artifact URL, Zenodo DOI slot (`TODO(zenodo-doi)`), title suffix |
| `references.bib` | `../paper/references.bib` plus two self-citations: the extended version and Paper 2 |
| `acmart.cls`, `pvldb.sty`, `ACM-Reference-Format.bst` | The official PVLDB template files, unmodified (see below) |
| `Makefile` | `make final` = stats + figures + pdf + page report. It runs Paper 1's tools from `../paper/tools`, which are not copied or modified |
| `generated/`, `figures/`, `main.pdf` | Build outputs (git-ignored, regenerated by `make final`) |

|---|---|
| Page limit [S] | EA&B papers: "up to 12 pages excluding references". "All content, including any appendices and acknowledgements but excluding the references, must fit on the given number of pages." There is no limit on reference pages. |
| Blinding [S] | "VLDB is a single-blind conference." Names and affiliations must be on the first page. |
| Title tag [S] | Append " [Experiment, Analysis & Benchmark]" to the title, "both in the paper file and in the CMT submission title". The tag is dropped in camera-ready. |
| EA&B expectations [C] | The track covers "extensive evaluation of algorithms, data structures, and systems that are of wide interest". Its value lies in new insights into the strengths and weaknesses of existing methods, new ways to evaluate them, or characterizing workloads. Experimental surveys compare multiple existing solutions, and reusable artifacts are core. |
| Artifact at submission [S] | New for Vol. 20: "already the initial submission must include a link to the full reproducibility package of all experiments, data, and artifacts with meaningful instructions on how to run the experiments." EA&B papers must make all experimental data and software available "(there are no excuses)" and must submit to the PVLDB Reproducibility Committee. A public GitHub repository is named as acceptable; personal web pages are not. |
| Template [F] | Strict adherence is required. "Any deviation (incl. omissions of mandatory blocks, such as copyright, reference format, artifact, etc.) will be liable to desk rejection." |
| Deadlines [S] | Deadlines fall on the 1st of each month, 5:00 PM Pacific Time. Abstract submission by the 25th of the previous month is mandatory. CMT opens on the 20th of the previous month. The first Vol. 20 deadline was 2026-04-01; the final one is 2027-03-01. |
| Reviewer nomination [S] | "every submission must nominate at least one of its own authors who is a qualified reviewer". Qualified means at least two previous research papers in SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE, EDBT or CIDR. If no author qualifies, nominate "the best-qualified author for consideration by the Editors-in-Chief". A nominated author may be asked to review at most 1 paper per month, and up to 3 per submitted paper. If they do not respond or review, every co-author's yearly cap drops by one. |
| Caps [S] | At most 2 submissions per author per month and 12 per year for Vol. 20. |
| Related submissions [S] | Concurrent related submissions by the same authors "must be explicitly cited, marked as 'under submission' in the list of references" and also reported in CMT. |
| Extended version / arXiv [P] | No page says anything specific about arXiv. The relevant wording is in [P]: "Issuing the paper as a technical report, posting the paper on a website, or presenting the paper at a workshop or conference that does not publish formally reviewed proceedings does not disqualify it from appearing in PVLDB." Overlap with a refereed prior publication must be cited. Nothing says reviewers must read an extended version, so the 12 pages are written to stand alone. |
| Conflicts [S] | Every author must enter domain and PC conflicts in CMT before the deadline. Undeclared or spurious conflicts lead to desk rejection. |

## What was kept, condensed or cut (relative to Paper 1)

- **Kept:** the problem, the contributions, and the RQ1–RQ4 results on the H100 (contrast table,
  headline times, per-query forest at SF300, speedup versus scale, crossover, coverage matrix).
  Also the ladder (SF300 contrast table and F11), integrity on every host, the key threats, the TPC
  scope notice and disclaimer (unchanged), and the artifact paragraph.
- **Condensed:** pre-registration (A1–A4 in one paragraph plus the commit and hash), research
  questions, arms (the Spark settings now fit in one table and one sentence), and the hosts
  section. The workload, sessions and the Williams square (now in the text, not a table), the
  gates and coverage rules, the statistical plan and the discussion are all condensed too. The
  threats are cut to four paragraphs.
- **Moved to the extended version, one sentence each here:** variability, order effects, startup,
  GPU memory, telemetry and plan variability. The exploratory E1/E4 comparison gets one sentence
  and points to Paper 2.
- **Cut:** the Spark configuration listing, the Latin-square table and the SF300 per-query
  median table. Also the legacy sums, the separate bridge table and figure (the bridge is now a
  column of Table 4), and the cross-host, ladder-coverage, telemetry and JAR tables. Also F3, F5–F9,
  F12 and F2x, the comparison with the earlier A100 study, the per-host paragraphs (two sentences
  remain), and both appendices.
